Each ring is one course description from the SCAD 2026–2027 catalog, ordered from 201 at the center to 792 at the edge. Thirty-seven design keywords carry their own hue, lighter as they recur, and a line joins every pair of matching words. Strategy courses, grounded in discourse, turn 36 degrees clockwise. Literacy courses, grounded in visual praxis, turn 72 degrees counterclockwise. Every line is redrawn at each step, and their movement together is Synthesis. Ten steps, ten posters, one generative system.
